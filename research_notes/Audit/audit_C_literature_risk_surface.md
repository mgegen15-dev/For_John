### 1a. Method spot-checks (literature map and risk flags)

**Retraction PMIDs.** All 418 unique PMIDs in `osteosarcoma_retracted_pmids.txt` match the live PubMed result for `osteosarcoma[tiab] AND retracted publication[pt]` exactly (418 = 418, no differences). CONFIRMED. (The file has 455 lines because of duplicates; see line 28.)

**Regex tags, 21-paper manual sample (seed 7, non-reviews 2005+).**

| Tag sampled | n | Tag correct under its own definition | Problems seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| cell_only | 7 | 5 | 28902349 and 35912730 used "osteosarcoma tissues" / "clinical tissues" (patient samples) but the patient regex only matches "tumour tissue", so they were tagged cell-only. 3 of 7 (27303058 BioID in U2OS, 32294496 plant scaffold, 18089713 PLK1 in colon/prostate lines) are not osteosarcoma research at all; U2OS is used as a generic cell-biology tool. |
| ncrna | 7 | 7 for "ncRNA" | 35992869 is a pure cell-line lncRNA "sponge" paper but tagged `patients=True` only because the intro says "osteosarcoma patients"; so it escapes the template. 31652459 is a review ("This review summarizes...") not flagged as review by PubMed type. |
| in_vivo | 7 | 4 | 17239409 (rat lung epithelial *cell line*), 24363062 (a letter with no abstract; "murine double minute" in title), 26170970 (a review of Src in sarcoma). |

**Quantified failure modes (recomputed over the whole dataset):**
- 328 of the 1,114 "template" papers (29%) say "OS tissues", "osteosarcoma tissues" or "clinical samples/specimens/tissues", i.e. they report patient tissue that the patient regex misses.
- Conversely, any abstract that says "patients" anywhere (e.g. "survival of osteosarcoma patients is poor") is tagged as having patient data; 852 of the 2,816 post-2010 ncRNA papers get a patient tag without animal or dog data, and 128 of those contain no tissue/sample/specimen/clinical word at all.
- 1,905 of 4,908 cell-only papers (39%), and 7,360 of 29,720 non-review papers (25%), do not mention osteosarcoma, OS, sarcoma or bone in the title: the corpus is "papers mentioning osteosarcoma (often only the U2OS cell line)", not "osteosarcoma research". This inflates denominators and the cell-only share.
- 141 non-review papers self-describe as a review or meta-analysis in the title/opening of the abstract.

**Gene normalisation, 10-gene sample (mention texts from PubTator):** correct for PTK2 ("FAK" 323), DKK1, CCNE1, PTH, MAP1LC3A ("LC3"). Wrong for H3P16 (all 357 mentions are "p21", i.e. CDKN1A), PTK2B (95/124 "protein kinase B" = AKT), TRP53-PS (421 "p53"), SLTM (243 "met" = MET receptor), TXK (121 "tyrosine kinase"), TENM1 (79 "TNM" staging), COX2 (206 "COX-2" = PTGS2), PGP ("P-gp" = ABCB1), ALB (42 "hsa", the miRNA prefix). 6 of 10 suspect genes sampled are mis-normalised; MET and PTGS2 are real drug targets whose literature is split.
