**Brief: summary of the biggest problem.** The central framing ("FAK has a drug already given to dogs, but no mouse evidence against metastasis") is wrong: a 2023 study found defactinib itself reduced lung metastasis in an orthotopic mouse model. And the DKK1 side omits that a canine DKK1 antisense drug is already being developed under NIH funding for dog trials.
