Verdicts: CONFIRMED, WRONG, OVERSTATED, UNVERIFIABLE, INTERNALLY INCONSISTENT (INCONSISTENT).

**Brief: summary of the biggest problem.** The central framing ("FAK has a drug already given to dogs, but no mouse evidence against metastasis") is wrong: a 2023 study found defactinib itself reduced lung metastasis in an orthotopic mouse model.
