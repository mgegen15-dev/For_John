## 1. Immune checkpoint inhibitors

### Takeaway
Single-agent PD-1 blockade gives roughly 0–5% responses in osteosarcoma (OS). PD-1 plus an anti-angiogenic TKI (camrelizumab + apatinib) reached about 21% ORR in China but missed its PFS target. PD-L1 expression and pulmonary-only disease are weak signals of benefit. No validated biomarker exists.

### Cited Findings
- SARC028 (NCT02301039), pembrolizumab 200 mg every 3 weeks: 1 objective response in 22 OS patients (5%). The primary ORR endpoint was not met in the bone cohort. Pre-treatment PD-L1 expression was infrequent but correlated with T-cell infiltration and response in UPS and OS. — [SARC028 Lancet Oncol abstract record](https://www.aminer.org/pub/5c0f708dda562944ac61c665/pembrolizumab-in-advanced-soft-tissue-sarcoma-and-bone-sarcoma-sarc-a-multicentre); [CT.gov results](https://classic.clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/results/NCT02301039)
- Alliance A091401, nivolumab ± ipilimumab in metastatic sarcoma: ORR 5% with nivolumab alone and 16% with the combination. The trial was not OS-specific, and its expansion cohorts (GIST, UPS, DDLPS) excluded OS. — [PMC6126546](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6126546); [A091401 expansion, JITC 2024](https://doaj.org/article/941ae913983049cc9a3aa2fd82cc1c48)
- Camrelizumab + apatinib, single-arm phase 2 (n=43, advanced OS after chemotherapy): 6-month PFS 50.9% (95% CI 34.6–65.0), ORR 20.9% (9/43). It missed the prespecified 6-month PFS target of ≥60%. Dose reductions or interruptions occurred in 55.8%. PD-L1 TPS ≥5% and lung-only metastases trended toward longer PFS. — [JITC 2020, PMC7223462](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7223462)
- A Chinese randomized trial is testing MAPI + camrelizumab vs API + apatinib vs MAPI in patients with a poor histologic response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy (front-line). — [trial listing](https://ctv.veeva.com/study/comparison-of-mapi-camrelizumab-versus-api-apatinib-versus-mapi-in-patients-with-a-poor-response-to)
