# Osteosarcoma Therapeutic Frontier (2018–2026)

Method note: based on ~14 web searches. Full-text fetches of fiercebiotech.com, cancernetwork.com and onclive.com failed — those hosts are not resolvable in this session's network settings — so a few 2025–2026 figures rest on search-result snippets of those pages and are flagged as such. Nothing below is from unsourced recall; where a source could not be retrieved it is listed under Gaps.

## Immune checkpoint inhibitors: results and why OS is "cold"

### Takeaway
Single-agent PD-1 blockade yields ~5% ORR in osteosarcoma and has never met a primary endpoint. The only combination with real signal is PD-1 plus an anti-angiogenic TKI (camrelizumab + apatinib, ORR 20.9%), which still missed its PFS target. No validated response biomarker exists; PD-L1 positivity is infrequent and only weakly associated with T-cell infiltration and response.

### Cited Findings
- SARC028 (NCT02301039), pembrolizumab 200 mg q3w, single-arm phase 2 at 12 SARC centers; bone cohort n=40 (OS, Ewing, dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma). 1/22 osteosarcoma patients (5%) had an objective response; the primary ORR endpoint was not met in either cohort. Activity was clearer in UPS and dedifferentiated liposarcoma. — [Lancet Oncol record](https://www.aminer.org/pub/5c0f708dda562944ac61c665/pembrolizumab-in-advanced-soft-tissue-sarcoma-and-bone-sarcoma-sarc-a-multicentre); [ClinicalTrials.gov results](https://classic.clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/results/NCT02301039)
- SARC028 correlative: pre-treatment PD-L1 expression was infrequent but correlated with T-cell infiltration and with response in UPS and OS. — [SARC028 record](https://www.aminer.org/pub/5c0f708dda562944ac61c665/pembrolizumab-in-advanced-soft-tissue-sarcoma-and-bone-sarcoma-sarc-a-multicentre)
- Alliance A091401 (nivolumab vs nivolumab + ipilimumab, metastatic sarcoma): 2 confirmed responses (5%) with nivolumab, 6 (16%) with the combination; authors called for a randomized study of the doublet. Expansion cohorts covered GIST, UPS and DDLPS and did **not** include osteosarcoma. The paper cites prior bone-sarcoma data (40 patients, only 2 responses across OS and chondrosarcoma). — [PMC6126546](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6126546); [expansion cohort, JITC 2024](https://doaj.org/article/941ae913983049cc9a3aa2fd82cc1c48)
- Camrelizumab (anti-PD-1) + apatinib (VEGFR2 TKI), single-arm open-label phase 2, n=43 advanced OS progressing after chemotherapy, enrolled Jan–Sep 2018: 6-month PFS 50.9% (95% CI 34.6–65.0); ORR 20.9% (9/43). Prespecified target of ≥60% 6-month PFS was not met. Dose reduction/interruption in 24/43 (55.8%). Patients with PD-L1 TPS ≥5% and those with pulmonary metastases trended to longer PFS. — [JITC 2020, PMC7223462](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7223462); [full text](https://jitc.bmj.com/content/8/1/e000798.full)
- A Chinese randomized front-line-adjacent trial compares MAPI + camrelizumab vs API (apatinib) vs MAPI in patients with poor histologic response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy. — [trial record](https://ctv.veeva.com/study/comparison-of-mapi-camrelizumab-versus-api-apatinib-versus-mapi-in-patients-with-a-poor-response-to)

### Inferences
- The PD-1 + VEGFR-TKI signal (ORR ~21% vs ~5% for PD-1 alone) suggests the benefit may come mostly from the TKI; no randomized trial has separated the two components in OS, which is a clear untested comparison.
- The lung-metastasis-trended-better observation in the camrelizumab/apatinib trial has, as far as retrieved sources show, never been prospectively tested as an enrichment strategy.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve a primary source quantifying why OS is immunologically "cold" (structural-variant/chromothripsis burden vs low TMB and low neoantigen load, MHC-I loss, macrophage-dominant infiltrate). This mechanistic framing needs a dedicated review citation (e.g. Nat Rev Clin Oncol / Cancer Cell immunotherapy-in-pediatric-sarcoma reviews) before being asserted.
- No retrieved source gives OS-specific nivolumab ± ipilimumab numbers from a dedicated OS cohort.

## Cellular therapies: CAR-T, engineered T cells, NK cells

### Takeaway
HER2 CAR-T is the most clinically mature (Baylor), with persistence and ~50% clinical benefit after lymphodepletion in HEROS 2.0, but no durable OS remissions. B7-H3 (CD276) is now the dominant target, and St. Jude has moved it into a phase 2 **consolidation** study in newly diagnosed metastatic OS — the first attempt to use CAR-T in minimal-residual rather than bulky relapsed disease.

### Cited Findings
- Ahmed et al. (JCO 2015) HER2 CAR-T: 19 patients with HER2+ tumors (16 osteosarcoma, 1 Ewing, 1 PNET, 1 DSRCT); given without lymphodepletion or IL-2, starting at ultra-low dose; CAR-T persisted ≥6 weeks in 7/9 evaluable patients receiving >1×10^6/m2. — [full-text PDF](https://typeset.io/pdf/human-epidermal-growth-factor-receptor-2-her2-specific-3odyr04640.pdf)
- HEROS 2.0 (phase 1, Texas Children's / Houston Methodist, published Nature Cancer 2024): 13 patients enrolled, 7 received multiple CAR-T infusions; lymphodepletion and repeat cycles improved CAR-T expansion and persistence; clinical benefit reported in 50% of treated patients. — [Baylor news](https://www.bcm.edu/news/car-t-cell-therapy-targeting-her2-antigen-shows-promise-against-advanced-sarcoma-in-phase-i-trial); [ecancer summary](https://ecancer.org/en/news/24633-car-t-cell-therapy-targeting-her2-antigen-shows-promise-against-advanced-sarcoma-in-phase-i-trial)
- The 2020 Nature Communications "exceptional response" to HER2 CAR-T was in metastatic **rhabdomyosarcoma**, not osteosarcoma; that patient remained disease-free >5 years. — [PMC7385999](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7385999)
- Osteosarcoma is described as HER2-positive (by IHC, typically low-level) in up to ~40% of patients. — [OncLive/Baylor coverage](https://www.bcm.edu/news/car-t-cell-therapy-targeting-her2-antigen-shows-promise-against-advanced-sarcoma-in-phase-i-trial)
- B7-H3 CAR-T open phase 1 trials include NCT04897321 and NCT06500819 (safety/MTD), and NCT04483778 combining B7-H3 CAR-T with CD19 CAR-T (now active, not recruiting). — [Front Immunol 2025 review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1586759/full)
- NCT07222735 (RAD3CAR, phase 1): B7-H3 CAR-T **after hypofractionated radiation** in patients ≤21 with relapsed/refractory B7-H3+ sarcomas including OS; primary objective safety; not yet recruiting at time of search. — [PMC12729273 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273); [trial record](https://www.syfrah.com/trial/NCT07222735)
- NCT07428993 (St. Jude, phase 2): B7-H3 CAR-T as **consolidation after upfront chemotherapy** in newly diagnosed high-risk metastatic osteosarcoma; estimated enrollment 41; start June 2026; primary completion December 2034. — [trial record](https://www.clinicaltrialsfinder.org/trials/nct07428993-evaluating-efficacy-of-b7-h3-car-t-cells-administered-at-the); [registry mirror](https://tundraspace.com/directory/studies/nct07428993)
- Published osteosarcoma CAR-T efficacy data remain largely preclinical; earlier GD2 and HER2 CAR-T phase 1 trials showed safety with only transient regression, with loss of response attributed to T-cell exhaustion and antigen heterogeneity. — [Front Immunol 2025 review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1586759/full)
- B7-H3-specific **engager (BiTE-like) T cells** for pediatric solid tumors are documented preclinically (Baylor). — [PMC4645489](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4645489/)
- C7R (constitutively active IL-7 receptor) appears in osteosarcoma T-cell-therapy review tables as an expansion-enhancing transgene; no OS clinical data were found. — [Front Immunol 2025 review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1586759/full)

### Inferences
- The field has shifted from "treat bulky relapse" to "consolidate micrometastatic disease" (NCT07428993) and to "condition the tumor first" (RAD3CAR radiation priming). These two design changes, not new antigens, are the most important 2026 developments.
- HER2 CAR-T's partial success with lymphodepletion plus repeat dosing suggests earlier CAR-T trials in OS were underdosed/under-conditioned rather than aimed at the wrong antigen — a reinterpretation that applies to the GD2 CAR-T experience too.

### Gaps
- No retrieved clinical data for EphA2, IL-11Rα, or GD2 CAR-T specifically in osteosarcoma patients (targets appear only in review tables).
- No retrieved current status for NK-cell (including NKG2D, allogeneic, or ex vivo expanded) programs in OS; Seattle Children's and Stanford OS-specific cell-therapy trial status could not be confirmed from the sources retrieved.
- No retrieved data on CAR-T delivery route in OS (intravenous vs intrapleural/inhaled/intra-arterial), despite the lung-restricted relapse pattern.

## GD2-targeted antibodies

### Takeaway
COG AOST1421 is the definitive negative GD2 antibody trial in osteosarcoma: dinutuximab + GM-CSF in surgically resected pulmonary recurrence gave a 12-month disease-control rate of 28.2%, failing its prespecified bar, and with meaningful toxicity including one sudden death. GD2 is expressed in OS but naked anti-GD2 does not work the way it does in neuroblastoma.

### Cited Findings
- AOST1421 (NCT02484443), single-arm phase 2, patients with recurrent pulmonary osteosarcoma in complete surgical remission; success threshold ≥16/39 event-free. Result: 11/39 event-free, DCR 28.2% (95% CI 15–44.9) against a historical 12-month benchmark of 20% (95% CI 10–34). Conclusion: no evidence of efficacy. — [COG publication record](https://childrensoncologygroup.org/publications/phase-2-study-of-anti-disialoganglioside-antibody-dinutuximab-in-combination-with-gm-csf-in-patients-with-recurrent-osteosarcoma-a-report-from-the-childrens-oncology-group/); [eScholarship full text](https://escholarship.org/content/qt8dx5s3gd/qt8dx5s3gd.pdf); Hingorani et al., Eur J Cancer 2022, PMID 35809374 — [record](https://curehunter.com/public/pubmed35809374.do)
- Toxicity: 1 of 136 administered cycles met unacceptable-toxicity criteria (sudden death of unknown cause); other ≥grade 3 events were pain, diarrhea, hypoxia, hypotension. — [eScholarship full text](https://escholarship.org/content/qt8dx5s3gd/qt8dx5s3gd.pdf)
- Protocol amendment: two SAEs (sudden death, grade 4 toxicity) were thought possibly related to higher peak plasma concentrations from the experimental 2-day infusion schedule; the study switched to a 4-day infusion. — [protocol/SAP PDF](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/43/NCT02484443/Prot_SAP_000.pdf)
- Dinutuximab PK and toxicity in AYA osteosarcoma patients were similar to younger patients. — [eScholarship full text](https://escholarship.org/content/qt8dx5s3gd/qt8dx5s3gd.pdf)
- Commentary (R. Lor Randall): anti-GD2 with dinutuximab "is not going to be a good standalone option for these patients." — [OncLive interview summary](https://www.onclive.com/view/dinutuximab-plus-gm-csf-does-not-improve-dcr-in-recurrent-osteosarcoma)

### Inferences
- The neuroblastoma-vs-OS contrast is informative: dinutuximab works in neuroblastoma as ADCC/CDC-dependent therapy in a minimal-residual setting alongside isotretinoin and cytokines, whereas in OS the same minimal-residual design failed — pointing to effector-cell/microenvironment deficiency (macrophage-rich, NK-poor, dense osteoid matrix) rather than lack of antigen.
- Because AOST1421 tested the *naked antibody in the best-case setting and still failed*, GD2 in OS is better pursued as a delivery handle (GD2 ADC, GD2 CAR-T, GD2 bispecific, GD2 radioligand) than as an ADCC agent. No retrieved source shows a GD2 ADC or GD2 radioligand clinical program in OS — a concrete unexploited gap.

### Gaps
- I found no published follow-up GD2 strategy in OS beyond the authors' statement that other GD2-targeting approaches are in development; no NCT identified.
- No retrieved head-to-head data on GD2 expression intensity/homogeneity in OS vs neuroblastoma.

## Antibody-drug conjugates and radioligands

### Takeaway
B7-H3 ADCs are the single most promising non-cellular modality in OS as of 2026: the Hansoh/GSK B7-H3 ADC (HS-20093 / ris-rez) produced ~17% ORR in relapsed/refractory OS in phase 2 and then a **positive randomized phase 3 in China** against gemcitabine/docetaxel. Glembatumumab vedotin (gpNMB) failed. HER2 ADC and radiopharmaceutical data in OS remain thin in retrieved sources.

### Cited Findings
- HS-20093 (B7-H3 ADC): 17.4% ORR reported in a phase 2 study in relapsed/refractory osteosarcoma. — [Springer 2025 review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44178-025-00217-w); [OncLive report](https://www.onclive.com/view/hs-20093-shows-promising-activity-in-relapsed-refractory-sarcoma) (fetch blocked; snippet only)
- A global phase 3 of the B7-H3 ADC began April 2025 (NCT05583255), comparing it with gemcitabine + docetaxel in patients progressing after second-line therapy. — [Springer 2025 review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44178-025-00217-w)
- ARTEMIS-002 (NCT05830123), phase 2, recruiting. — [PMC12729273 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273)
- ARTEMIS-011 (China, randomized phase 3) of ris-rez (GSK/Hansoh B7-H3 ADC): 122 patients aged ≥12 who failed second-line treatment; reported a 67% reduction in risk (PFS) at median follow-up 7.1 months. **Press/news-reported, not peer-reviewed.** GSK's global Embold Sarcoma-202 phase 1b/2 also includes osteosarcoma in previously treated unresectable advanced/metastatic sarcoma. — [FierceBiotech report](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsks-b7-h3-adc-drives-67-cancer-risk-reduction-phase-3) (fetch blocked; search snippet only); [second FierceBiotech item](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsk-partner-hansoh-reports-another-phase-3-win-priority-adc)
- Ifinatamab deruxtecan (I-DXd, DS-7300, B7-H3 ADC, Daiichi Sankyo/Merck): a global phase 1/2 began in July, with part 2 enrolling osteosarcoma among other tumor types. No registry detail (enrollment, dosing) was retrievable. — [search finding, Springer/PMC reviews above]
- Glembatumumab vedotin (gpNMB ADC) in recurrent osteosarcoma: COG AOST1521, NCT02487979, listed as Active – Closed to Enrollment. **No response or outcome data were retrievable** from the sources searched. — [trial identification via search; CT.gov record NCT02487979]
- Preclinical caution: the 92.3% response figure sometimes quoted for the B7-H3 ADC m276-SL-PBD comes from pediatric **xenograft** models, not patients. — [Front Immunol 2025 review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1586759/full)
- A 2025 review states no clinical results have been reported in osteosarcoma patients for B7-H3 targeting combined with immunotherapy. — [PMC12729273 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273)

### Inferences
- Two independent B7-H3 ADC programs (Hansoh/GSK and Daiichi/Merck) plus multiple B7-H3 CAR-T trials mean B7-H3 is now the most crowded target in OS. The unexploited angle is **B7-H3 ADC plus checkpoint inhibitor or plus B7-H3 CAR-T sequencing**, which reviews explicitly say has no clinical data.
- The B7-H3 phase 3 ran in a late-line, adult-inclusive (≥12 y) Chinese population; the untested question is B7-H3 ADC in the adjuvant/micrometastatic setting, where the modality's lung penetration matters.

### Gaps
- Glembatumumab vedotin AOST1521 efficacy numbers could not be retrieved; this is a documented negative trial in the field but I have no citable response rate.
- Radium-223 and samarium-153-EDTMP in osteosarcoma: **no sources retrieved**. I cannot report response rates, n, or trial identifiers for bone-seeking radiopharmaceuticals, nor for newer targeted radioligands (e.g. FAP- or B7-H3-directed). This is a real evidence gap in these notes, not a claim of absence of literature.
- Trastuzumab deruxtecan in osteosarcoma: no OS-specific trial or result retrieved.

## Multi-kinase TKIs

### Takeaway
VEGFR-directed multi-kinase TKIs are the only class with reproducible activity in relapsed OS — regorafenib is clearly active on a progression-free endpoint, cabozantinib gives ~12% ORR — but no randomized trial has shown an OS (survival) benefit, and the one attempt to add a TKI to salvage chemotherapy (OLIE) missed. The class is now being tested front-line for the first time in COG AOST2032.

### Cited Findings
- REGOBONE (regorafenib, randomized double-blind placebo-controlled phase 2, adults with metastatic OS): 17/26 (65%) non-progressive at 8 weeks on regorafenib vs 0 on placebo. Serious treatment-related AEs in 7/29 (24%) vs 0/14. — [Lancet Oncol 2019 record](https://read.qxmd.com/read/30477937/efficacy-and-safety-of-regorafenib-in-adult-patients-with-metastatic-osteosarcoma-a-non-comparative-randomised-double-blind-placebo-controlled-phase-2-study)
- CABONE (cabozantinib, multicentre single-arm phase 2 in Ewing sarcoma or osteosarcoma): in the OS cohort, 5 objective responses, ORR 11.9%; 41% of patients had some reduction in tumor burden. A later real-world study reported a similar AE profile. — [Targeted Oncology summary](https://targetedonc.com/view/cabozantinib-prolongs-survival-in-patients-with-advanced-osteosarcoma-and-ewing-sarcoma); [PMC9918141](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9918141)
- OLIE (NCT04154189), randomized phase 2, lenvatinib + ifosfamide/etoposide (LEN-IE) vs IE, ages 2–25, relapsed/refractory high-grade OS: n=81 (40 LEN-IE, 41 IE); median age 15.0 y (IQR 12–18); 46 males (56.8%). Median PFS 6.5 months (95% CI 5.7–8.2) vs 5.5 months (95% CI 2.9–6.5); HR 0.54 (95% CI 0.27–1.08), 1-sided P=.04 — did not meet the prespecified significance threshold. Crossover to lenvatinib was permitted on progression. Median OS was reported as longer in the **IE (control)** arm. Published JAMA Oncology, December 2024. — [CT.gov NCT04154189](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04154189); [ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/news/october-2024/novel-treatment-options-for-children-and-young-adults-with-relapsed-osteosarcoma/); [CureToday](https://www.curetoday.com/view/lenvima-combo-misses-end-goal-in-children-with-osteosarcoma); [record](https://ouci.dntb.gov.ua/works/4MdD22z7)
- Apatinib: combined with camrelizumab gave ORR 20.9% / 6-month PFS 50.9% (see checkpoint section); authors noted the combination appeared to prolong PFS relative to single-agent apatinib. — [PMC7223462](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7223462)
- AOST2032 (NCT05691478), COG phase 2/3: cabozantinib added to MAP in **newly diagnosed** high-grade osteosarcoma. Design: feasibility/dose-finding phase, then randomization to MAP + surgery vs cabozantinib + MAP + surgery, stratified by risk (standard risk vs high risk). Co-primary endpoints feasibility and event-free survival. Eligibility: newly diagnosed high-grade OS, age <40 (one listing says 0–40; another erroneously lists ≥18), no prior systemic therapy, no CNS metastases; one listing excludes medication-requiring hypertension ≥140/90. Status: recruiting; per Cedars-Sinai, as of Amendment 2B the feasibility phase is complete. — [St. Jude](https://www.stjude.org/treatment/clinical-trials/cabozantinib-with-chemotherapy-for-osteosarcoma.html); [Cedars-Sinai AOST2032](https://clinicaltrials.cedars-sinai.edu/view/AOST2032); [Roswell Park](https://www.roswellpark.org/clinical-trials/list/5225); [cancervic record](https://trials.cancervic.org.au/details/vctl_nct05691478)

### Inferences
- OLIE's "longer OS in the control arm despite better PFS on lenvatinib" pattern, combined with 55.8% dose modification in the camrelizumab/apatinib study, suggests TKI toxicity and post-progression effects, not lack of antitumor activity, are what block the class. Intermittent/lower-dose or maintenance-only schedules are an obvious untested design.
- AOST2032 is the single highest-stakes OS trial currently running: if cabozantinib + MAP fails on EFS, the entire VEGFR hypothesis in OS loses its front-line route.
- REGOBONE's dramatic 65% vs 0% 8-week non-progression but modest ORR means progression-free-at-landmark endpoints overstate TKI benefit relative to RECIST response; cross-trial comparisons of "activity" in OS are badly confounded by endpoint choice (single-arm ORR vs landmark PFS).

### Gaps
- Sorafenib ± everolimus (Italian Sarcoma Group) and anlotinib OS-specific results were not retrieved; no citable numbers.
- No retrieved information on whether regorafenib or cabozantinib is moving into any randomized maintenance trial after front-line therapy outside AOST2032.

## Other targeted and novel agents

### Takeaway
The strongest 2026 signal outside B7-H3 is OST-HER2 (ADXS31-164 / OST31-164), a HER2-directed live-attenuated *Listeria* vaccine developed through canine comparative oncology, which reported a final 3-year OS of 72.7% vs 45.8% in combined historical controls in pulmonary metastatic OS — but this is company-reported, historically controlled, and not peer-reviewed. Zoledronic acid is definitively negative.

### Cited Findings
- OS2006 (NCT00470223), randomized, n=318, chemotherapy ± zoledronate: 3-year EFS 57.1% (95% CI 48.8–65.0) with zoledronate vs 63.4% (55.2–70.9) control, HR 1.36, P=.094; 3-year OS 73.4% vs 84.4%. Excess grade ≥3 hypocalcemia 29% vs 6% and hypophosphatemia 40% vs 17%. Authors: "we do not recommend zoledronate in osteosarcoma patients." — [CancerNetwork summary](https://cancernetwork.com/view/zoledronate-fails-improve-outcomes-chemo-osteosarcoma) (fetch blocked; snippet); [ASCO Post](https://www.ascopost.com/News/42704); [CT.gov NCT00470223](https://classic.clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/results/NCT00470223); [EU CTR 2006-003377-27](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2006-003377-27/FR)
- Canine ADXS31-164 phase 1: attenuated recombinant *Listeria monocytogenes* expressing a chimeric human HER2/neu fusion, given IV to dogs with HER2/neu+ osteosarcoma; toxicities low grade and transient. In the 18-dog report, it broke peripheral tolerance and induced antigen-specific IFN-γ responses against the HER2/neu intracellular domain in 15/18 dogs within 6 months, reduced incidence of metastatic disease, and significantly increased survival vs a historical control group — described by the authors as preliminary from a small uncontrolled comparison. — [Drexel record](https://researchdiscovery.drexel.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Immunotherapy-with-a-HER2-Targeting-Listeria-Induces/991019302291804721); [PMC4288668](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4288668); [aminer record](https://aminer.org/pub/56f0014c0cf276b2162583a6/immunotherapy-with-a-her-targeted-listeria-induces-her-specific-immunity-and-demonstrates)
- A follow-on canine study randomizes dogs ineligible for amputation to ADXS31-164 vs placebo alongside palliative or stereotactic radiation. — [canine trial record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8909565)
- Human OST31-164 / OST-HER2 phase 2 maintenance: up to 45 patients aged 12–39 with osteosarcoma recurrent in the lung and recently surgically resected; infusions every 3 weeks over 48 weeks. — [Lurie Children's trial page](https://research.luriechildrens.org/en/clinical-studies-search/osteosarcoma-maintenance-therapy-with-ost31-164/); [Johns Hopkins IRB00319000](https://hopkinsmedicine.org/clinical-trials/results/IRB00319000)
- October 2026 report: final 3-year OS 72.7% with OST-HER2 vs 45.8% in a combined historical control, in a phase 2b trial in pulmonary metastatic osteosarcoma; sponsor preparing a confirmatory phase 3. **Company press-release-derived, unreviewed.** — [CancerNetwork, Oct 7 2026](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/ost-her2-shows-significant-final-3-year-os-benefit-in-osteosarcoma-trial) (fetch blocked; search snippet only)
- Inhaled cytokine delivery: UC Davis phase 1 of inhaled recombinant IL-15 in dogs with pulmonary metastases from osteosarcoma or melanoma established 50 µg inhaled twice daily × 14 days as the recommended phase 2 dose (J Immunother Cancer 2022). — [canine inhaled IL-15, escholarship](https://escholarship.org/content/qt7d7091vm/qt7d7091vm.pdf?t=rffogz)
- B7-H3 biology and therapeutic strategies in OS are reviewed in detail, including CAR-T, ADC, bispecific and engager formats. — [PMC12729273](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273); [Exploiting B7-H3 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273)

### Inferences
- OST-HER2 is the clearest comparative-oncology-to-human translation success story in OS, and it validates the *resected pulmonary metastasis / maintenance* setting as the place where immunotherapy in OS can show an effect — exactly the setting where dinutuximab failed. The contrast (vaccine works, antibody doesn't, same disease state) argues the limiting factor is T-cell priming, not antibody delivery.
- Historical-control design is the main weakness: a 45.8% 3-year OS control is plausible but the comparison is not randomized, and the field has been burned before by historically-controlled OS results.
- Inhaled delivery has only been formally dose-found in **dogs** (IL-15). Inhaled immunotherapy in human OS appears to be an open, essentially unoccupied space.

### Gaps
- No sources retrieved for OS-specific results of: CDK4/6 inhibitors, mTOR inhibitors (beyond the sorafenib/everolimus gap above), PARP + ATR inhibitor combinations, WEE1 inhibitors, MDM2 inhibitors, denosumab/RANKL, mifamurtide (MTP-PE), oncolytic viruses, or macrophage-directed therapy (CSF1R, CD47, macrophage re-education). I have **no citable numbers** for any of these; they must be researched separately before the report asserts anything about them. This is the largest hole in these notes.

## Metastasis-directed strategies

### Takeaway
Retrieved evidence here is thin. The one well-documented fact is that the resected-pulmonary-metastasis minimal-residual-disease setting has been formally used as a trial platform (AOST1421, OST31-164 phase 2, and now St. Jude's B7-H3 CAR-T consolidation), with one failure, one positive-but-historically-controlled result, and one trial just starting.

### Cited Findings
- AOST1421 enrolled specifically patients with recurrent pulmonary osteosarcoma **in complete surgical remission** — i.e. a micrometastatic/MRD design — and failed (DCR 28.2%). — [eScholarship](https://escholarship.org/content/qt8dx5s3gd/qt8dx5s3gd.pdf)
- OST31-164 phase 2 uses the same design: lung recurrence, recently surgically resected, 48 weeks of maintenance. — [Lurie Children's](https://research.luriechildrens.org/en/clinical-studies-search/osteosarcoma-maintenance-therapy-with-ost31-164/)
- NCT07428993 applies B7-H3 CAR-T as consolidation after upfront chemotherapy in newly diagnosed **metastatic** high-risk OS. — [trial record](https://www.clinicaltrialsfinder.org/trials/nct07428993-evaluating-efficacy-of-b7-h3-car-t-cells-administered-at-the)
- Inhaled delivery to the pulmonary metastatic niche has a completed canine dose-finding study (inhaled IL-15, RP2D 50 µg BID × 14 days). — [escholarship](https://escholarship.org/content/qt7d7091vm/qt7d7091vm.pdf?t=rffogz)

### Inferences
- Three different modalities have now been aimed at the identical clinical state (resected lung metastases). That makes this state the de facto standard platform for OS immunotherapy — and means a randomized trial in this setting comparing modalities, rather than another single-arm historical comparison, is the obvious missing study.

### Gaps
- No sources retrieved on: the lung metastatic niche biology, tumor-cell dormancy in OS, ezrin, metastasis-suppressor pathways, or SBRT/oligometastatic ablation trials in OS. I found nothing citable and will not speculate. These need a dedicated search pass.

## Open trial pipeline as of late 2026

### Takeaway
The 2026 pipeline is dominated by B7-H3 (one global phase 3 ADC, one Chinese positive phase 3, multiple CAR-T phase 1/2), one front-line randomized TKI trial (AOST2032), and one vaccine heading to confirmatory phase 3 (OST-HER2). China is a major contributor of randomized OS trials.

### Cited Findings
- Randomized / front-line: **AOST2032** (NCT05691478) cabozantinib + MAP, newly diagnosed, phase 2/3, co-primary feasibility and EFS, recruiting, feasibility phase complete per Amendment 2B. — [Cedars-Sinai](https://clinicaltrials.cedars-sinai.edu/view/AOST2032); [St. Jude](https://www.stjude.org/treatment/clinical-trials/cabozantinib-with-chemotherapy-for-osteosarcoma.html)
- Randomized, late-line: **NCT05583255**, global phase 3 of the B7-H3 ADC vs gemcitabine/docetaxel after second-line progression, started April 2025. — [Springer 2025 review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44178-025-00217-w)
- Randomized, China: **ARTEMIS-011**, ris-rez phase 3, n=122, age ≥12, post-second-line, reported positive (news-reported). — [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsks-b7-h3-adc-drives-67-cancer-risk-reduction-phase-3) (snippet only)
- Randomized, China, front-line-adjacent: MAPI + camrelizumab vs API vs MAPI in poor neoadjuvant responders. — [trial record](https://ctv.veeva.com/study/comparison-of-mapi-camrelizumab-versus-api-apatinib-versus-mapi-in-patients-with-a-poor-response-to)
- Phase 2, single-arm: **ARTEMIS-002** (NCT05830123) recruiting; **Embold Sarcoma-202** phase 1b/2 (GSK, global, includes OS); I-DXd global phase 1/2 part 2 includes OS. — [PMC12729273](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273); [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsk-partner-hansoh-reports-another-phase-3-win-priority-adc)
- Cell therapy: **NCT07428993** (St. Jude phase 2 consolidation, start June 2026, n=41 est., primary completion Dec 2034); **NCT07222735** (RAD3CAR, radiation + B7-H3 CAR-T, ≤21 y, not yet recruiting); **NCT04897321**, **NCT06500819** (phase 1 B7-H3 CAR-T); **NCT04483778** (B7-H3 + CD19 CAR-T, active not recruiting). — [Front Immunol 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2025.1586759/full); [PMC12729273](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273)
- Vaccine: OST31-164 phase 2 (up to 45 patients, 12–39 y, resected lung recurrence) with a confirmatory phase 3 in preparation per sponsor. — [Lurie Children's](https://research.luriechildrens.org/en/clinical-studies-search/osteosarcoma-maintenance-therapy-with-ost31-164/); [CancerNetwork Oct 2026](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/ost-her2-shows-significant-final-3-year-os-benefit-in-osteosarcoma-trial) (snippet only)
- Closed/negative: AOST1421 (dinutuximab, negative), AOST1521 / NCT02487979 (glembatumumab vedotin, active–closed to enrollment, results not retrieved), OS2006 (zoledronate, negative), SARC028 bone cohort (negative), OLIE (missed primary endpoint). — citations above

### Inferences
- The pipeline's center of gravity has moved from checkpoint inhibitors (2016–2020, uniformly negative) to B7-H3-directed payload delivery (2023–2026). Checkpoint monotherapy is effectively dead in OS.
- Almost all positive-looking 2026 results are either single-arm, historically controlled, or press-released. The field's credibility problem is design, not drugs.

### Gaps
- I did not run direct ClinicalTrials.gov or EU CTR bulk searches (the registry aggregator pages retrieved were secondary). A systematic registry query for "osteosarcoma, recruiting, interventional" would give a complete and verifiable trial count; the list above is representative, not exhaustive.
- No retrieved information on SARC's or the EU consortia's (FaR-RMS-equivalent, EURO-B.O.S.S., OS2006 successor) current open OS trials.

## Cross-cutting gaps and overlooked angles (for the gap-hunting objective)

### Signals never followed up
- PD-L1 TPS ≥5% and pulmonary-only metastases both trended to longer PFS on camrelizumab + apatinib; no prospective biomarker-enriched trial has been reported. — [PMC7223462](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7223462)
- SARC028's correlation of PD-L1 with T-cell infiltration and response in OS was never used to select patients in a follow-on OS trial. — [SARC028 record](https://www.aminer.org/pub/5c0f708dda562944ac61c665/pembrolizumab-in-advanced-soft-tissue-sarcoma-and-bone-sarcoma-sarc-a-multicentre)
- Canine ADXS31-164 reduced metastatic incidence and extended survival in 2014–2016 and only reached a human phase 2b a decade later; the canine radiation + vaccine randomized study has no human analogue. — [PMC4288668](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4288668); [canine follow-on](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8909565)
- Inhaled IL-15 completed canine dose-finding in 2022 with a defined RP2D and no retrieved human OS trial. — [escholarship](https://escholarship.org/content/qt7d7091vm/qt7d7091vm.pdf?t=rffogz)

### Combinations explicitly not yet tried in patients
- B7-H3-directed therapy + checkpoint inhibitor: a 2025 review states no clinical results exist in OS patients. — [PMC12729273](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12729273)
- B7-H3 ADC in the adjuvant/MRD (resected lung metastasis) setting rather than late-line.
- GD2 as a payload-delivery handle (ADC, radioligand, bispecific) rather than naked ADCC antibody, given AOST1421's failure of the naked antibody in the best-case setting.
- Separating the TKI from the PD-1 antibody in the camrelizumab/apatinib result — no randomized deconvolution exists.
- CAR-T delivered regionally to the lung, despite lung-restricted relapse and the HER2/B7-H3 trafficking problem.

### Lessons from failures
- Minimal-residual-disease designs are feasible and have been used three times; a 12-month DCR historical benchmark of ~20% (95% CI 10–34) is the published control for resected pulmonary recurrence, which is wide enough that single-arm trials in this setting are poorly powered — AOST1421's 28.2% was numerically above the benchmark and still called negative. — [eScholarship](https://escholarship.org/content/qt8dx5s3gd/qt8dx5s3gd.pdf)
- Adding an agent to salvage chemotherapy can improve PFS and worsen OS (OLIE), so PFS-primary designs in relapsed OS are unreliable. — [CureToday](https://www.curetoday.com/view/lenvima-combo-misses-end-goal-in-children-with-osteosarcoma)
- Adding a bone-targeted agent to front-line chemotherapy actively harmed outcomes (OS2006: EFS HR 1.36, OS 73.4% vs 84.4%), a caution for any front-line addition including AOST2032. — [ASCO Post](https://www.ascopost.com/News/42704)
- Under-conditioned, single-dose CAR-T in 2015 looked inactive; the same antigen with lymphodepletion and repeat dosing gave 50% clinical benefit in 2024 — negative early cell-therapy trials in OS should not be read as negative antigens. — [Baylor](https://www.bcm.edu/news/car-t-cell-therapy-targeting-her2-antigen-shows-promise-against-advanced-sarcoma-in-phase-i-trial)
