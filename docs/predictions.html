Predictions Log

Started November 22, 2026. This is a public, dated record of what For John predicts, written before the answers are known. When trials, studies or reviewers report, each prediction will be marked right, wrong or unresolved. Entries are never edited after the fact; corrections and outcomes are added below each one with a date.

Why keep this. Anyone can explain results after they arrive. Committing to predictions in advance is the honest test of whether connecting the published evidence, as this project does, finds real signals. If our hit rate is poor, that is worth knowing too.

How to read it. Each prediction states what we expect, what would prove it wrong, how and roughly when it could be tested, and our confidence: a rough probability that it turns out right, which is our judgement, not a calculation. Most are hypotheses for researchers to test. Not medical advice.

Outcomes

Each outcome is added here with its date, source and verdict (right, wrong or unresolved), without changing the prediction above.

  • P15, 2026-11-24: unresolved by its own rule. Pooled hazard ratio per SD 0.99 (95% CI 0.74–1.32) across three independent cohorts (110 patients, 53 events), against 2.30 in the discovery cohort. Not right (no association seen); not wrong by the stated rule, because the upper bound is 1.32, not below 1.3. The discovery-size effect is ruled out. Source: reports/Engine cycle 1 - does RAMP1 predict relapse.md (our calculation).
  • P16, 2026-11-24: unresolved by its own rule. CXCR4 RNA in lung metastases against primary tumours, six datasets, 29 against 51: +0.43 standard deviations (95% CI −0.05 to +0.91). Not right (the lower bound is below 0); not wrong by the stated rule (the upper bound is above 0.5). The lean disappeared after adjusting for immune cells (+0.06). Source: reports/Engine cycle 2 - do lung metastases carry more CXCR4.md (our calculation).
  • P17, 2026-11-24: unresolved by its own rule. CXCL12 RNA, same comparison: −0.08 standard deviations (−0.55 to +0.39). No decrease seen; not wrong by the stated rule, because the lower bound is −0.55, not above −0.5. Same source.
  • P18, 2026-11-24: unresolved by its own rule. CDKN2A deep deletion, metastasis or relapse against primary, two cohorts, 130 against 211 patients: pooled odds ratio 1.10 (95% CI 0.59–2.06). Not right (the lower bound is below 1); not wrong by the stated rule (the upper bound is above 1.5). Source: reports/Engine cycle 3 - is 9p21 deletion more common at relapse.md (our calculation).
